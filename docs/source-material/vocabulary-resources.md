## Daily Vocabulary Routine (~30–40 min)
Split: ~60% IELTS/academic, ~40% everyday/workplace.

1. **Anki review — 10–15 min (daily, non-negotiable).** Do due cards first; cap new cards at ~10–15/day.
2. **AWL block — 5–10 min.** One sublist per 1–2 weeks (start Sublist 1). Learn word families. Weekly: run a text through the AWL Highlighter.
3. **IELTS topic vocab — 5 min.** Rotate IELTS Liz topics. Capture **collocations**, not lone words ("tackle unemployment", "sedentary lifestyle").
4. **Everyday/workplace — 5–10 min.** Mon/Wed/Fri: one BBC "The English We Speak". Tue/Thu: one BBC News Review. Weekend: British Council idioms / Phrase-ology e-book.
5. **Weekly consolidation — 20 min.** Review week's cards, delete duds. Write a paragraph or speak 2 min using 8–10 new words.

**Card format:** front = word/phrase; back = short definition + one example sentence + key collocation. Prioritise phrases/collocations over lone words — that's the band-8 differentiator.
